## Context

For a university assignment, the goal was simple to state and harder to achieve: write letters that editors would choose to publish.

## The challenge

Each piece needed a clear point of view, a concise structure and a subject relevant to a particular newspaper and its readers. Publication—not just submission—was the measure of whether the writing connected.

## The outcome

Five letters were published, covering bullying, fear, education credentials, pollution and tourism in Barcelona:

- *Bullying*, in Diario Bahía de Cádiz
- *Fear and hiding*, in La Nueva España
- *Títulos de papel mojado*, in La Nueva España
- *Contaminación*, in Faro de Vigo
- *Barcelonaland*, in El Periódico

The exercise reinforced the value of adapting an argument to its context while keeping the writer’s own voice intact.
