## Context

A postgraduate exercise in writing for real constraints across email and SMS.

## Approach

The three emails covered a missed deadline, a hiring task and lost shoes. The three text messages covered a bank card, an identity-document appointment and help numbers in France. I adjusted formality, detail and emotional tone for each relationship and channel.

## Learning

Apologies need a solution, encouragement needs clarity and short messages still need enough context to be trusted.
