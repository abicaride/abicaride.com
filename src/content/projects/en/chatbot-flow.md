## Context

This postgraduate exercise mapped two happy paths for a fictional streaming assistant: recommending something to watch and helping with a problem.

## Approach

I considered the assistant’s personality, the power relationship in the conversation and how to reach a useful outcome with as little cognitive fatigue as possible.

## Learning

A conversational interface should feel human enough to be clear, but never so performative that it delays the answer.
