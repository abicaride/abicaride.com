## Context

A postgraduate exercise in treating metadata as part of the user experience, not as invisible technical copy.

## Approach

I researched search results and relevant terms, including with Semrush, for three subjects: remote-work exercises, Notion templates and car-insurance excess. I then proposed titles and descriptions and explained the reasoning behind each choice.

## Learning

The same format needs a different promise for every intent. Useful search copy sets expectations before the visit begins.
