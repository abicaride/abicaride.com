## Approved working visual direction

- **Direction:** Clean Organic Editorial — Pre-production
- **Hero:** positioning and calls to action followed by a large, full-width
  landscape treatment of `WhatsApp Image 2026-08-25 at 18.49.46`
- **Photography:** no physical-appearance retouching; photography receives equal
  visual importance to the positioning without forcing a literal two-column
  split
- **Headings:** Inter
- **Body:** Montserrat Regular with generous line height and comfortable measure
- **Primary color:** green
- **Supporting color:** very pale pink and blue, used sparingly
- **Base:** warm neutral backgrounds
- **Avoid:** red, orange, purple, rainbow/pastel-block layouts and floating
  circles or ellipses
- **Section rhythm:** whitespace, thin rules and restrained full-width background
  changes rather than repeated cards or colored containers
- **Asymmetry:** subtle and editorial only; never at the expense of hierarchy
- **Curves:** limited to functional image radii, CTA shapes or purposeful crops
