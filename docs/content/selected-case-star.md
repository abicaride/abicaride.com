## imaginArt

### Situation

imaginArt operates in a specialist B2B audiovisual environment where technical
information had to remain accurate while becoming clearer and more useful to
customers.

### Task

Turn technical and commercial input into communication that helped professional
audiences understand products, make decisions and act across different
channels.

### Action

Abilene worked between Engineering, Sales and Management. She structured
information, defined hierarchy and framing, wrote copy and executed
communication across web, email, events and commercial materials.

### Result

Supported evidence includes:

- an approximate recalled newsletter open rate moving from **~24% to ~34%**;
- approximate recalled Madrid Open Days attendance of **~110–125**, compared
  with a usual **~70–80** range for similar events;
- reusable structured approaches to technical product communication.

The quantitative figures are approximate recollections. The newsletter was not
an A/B test, and no single editorial change is presented as causal. The event
comparison does not establish a precise uplift or prove that the communication
alone caused attendance. Preserve these caveats unless stronger documentary
evidence becomes available.

## Cognitive Biases

### Situation

An ecommerce marketplace uses many interface patterns that can influence
purchase decisions.

### Task

Analyse the experience through behavioural-design principles and identify how
specific patterns might affect user decisions.

### Action

Abilene identified patterns, documented where they appeared, explained how they
worked and considered their likely effect on decision-making.

### Result

A structured behavioural audit demonstrating pattern recognition and critical
analysis of whether a design supports a decision or merely pushes it.

## Error Messages

### Situation

Error states create friction when users need to understand what happened and
how to continue.

### Task

Solve common error scenarios while balancing clarity, recovery guidance and
distinct brand voices.

### Action

Abilene wrote different solutions for four brands, adapting vocabulary, tone
and personality without hiding the practical next step.

### Result

A set of error messages demonstrating clear recovery guidance across four
different brand voices.
